How to pair two devices

  1. Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot.
  2. Device A taps Create Connection to gather local network ICE candidates and show an Offer QR code.
  3. Device B taps Scan QR Code, scans Device A's QR code(s), and automatically displays an Answer QR code.
  4. Device A taps Scan QR Code to scan Device B's Answer QR code(s) and establish the direct link.

Note: Some public Wi-Fi routers and phone hotspots enable client isolation, which blocks direct peer-to-peer connections between devices.
